Title: Burnout
Zubaan: Simple English
Kab kaam aata: When a person has long-term work-related stress and feels exhausted, mentally distant from work, or less effective at work.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
WHO describes burnout specifically in the workplace context. It is related to chronic workplace stress that has not been successfully managed. This means that not every kind of tiredness or study pressure should be called burnout.

Burnout is described through three main areas: feeling exhausted or having low energy, feeling mentally distant or negative about work, and feeling less effective at work.

It may help to notice the causes of work-related stress and, where possible, talk about workload, communication, or support with a supervisor or trusted colleague. Rest, breaks, and time away from work may also support wellbeing.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If work-related stress is difficult to manage and exhaustion or emotional difficulties start affecting daily functioning, talking to a healthcare professional or using appropriate workplace support may help.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not label every tired person as having burnout. Do not use burnout as a general label for all stress outside the workplace.
