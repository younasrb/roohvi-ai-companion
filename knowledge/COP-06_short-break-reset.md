ID: COP-06
Title: Chhota break / reset
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: Jab user kaam ya padhai ke beech thak jaye ya dimaag bhar jaye.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
NHS din ke beech chhote breaks lene ka mashwara deta hai, aur kaam se pehle, beech me ya baad me thori der chalne ka bhi. Mayo Clinic kehta hai ke subah ya dopahar ke break me harkat karein, stretch karein ya chal kar aayen. Is liye jab thakan ya bojh mehsoos ho: apni kursi ya kaam se uth jayein, kuch minute ke liye thori der chalein, gardan, kandhe aur haath aahista stretch karein. Phir wapas aane se pehle apne liye sirf agla ek chhota kaam tay karein, poora din nahi. Break ka matlab kaam chhodna nahi, dobara taza hona hai. NHS yeh bhi kehta hai ke kaam ka waqt khatam hone par kaam se door hona aur notifications band karna madad karta hai. Waqt: 5 se 10 minute. Break ke baad wapas aayen, taake break masle se bachne ka tareeqa na ban jaye. Yeh ilaaj nahi, ek chhoti technique hai jo kuch logon ko pur-sukoon mehsoos karne me madad karti hai.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Agar thakan ya dabaao lagataar rahe, aur roz ke kaam ya zehni sehat par asar dale, to NHS ke mutabiq doctor se baat karein. Kisi bharosemand insaan se apni baat karna bhi madad kar sakta hai. Break ko har waqt masle se bachne ke liye istemal na karein. Regular break ke bawajood dabaao barhta rahe to baat karein.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Aam break ko 'bimari' ka ishara na batayein.

ID: COP-06
Title: Short reset break
Zubaan: English
Kab kaam aata: When the user is tired or overloaded mid-task.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
NHS recommends short breaks during the day and a walk before, during or after work. Mayo Clinic suggests using a break to move, stretch or walk. Step away, walk a little, stretch your neck, shoulders and hands, then decide only the next small task.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If tiredness or stress keeps going and affects daily life or mental health, talk to a GP/doctor.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not describe an ordinary break as a sign of illness.
