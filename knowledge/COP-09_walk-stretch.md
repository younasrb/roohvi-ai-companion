Title: Thori der chalna ya stretch
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: Jab user ka dil ghabraye, mood bhaari ho ya woh der se baitha ho.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
NHS ke mutabiq hilna jhulna, stretch karna ya jism ko harkat dena jazbaati bojh se nikalne me madad kar sakta hai, aur chhoti si sair bhi kaam ke dauran ya baad me zehni break ban sakti hai. Active 10 ke mutabiq roz 10 minute se shuru karna kaafi hai, aur har minute ginta hai. Mayo Clinic kehta hai ke din me kai 10 minute ki sairein bhi asar rakhti hain. Is liye aaram se chalein, apni raftar par, ghar ke andar ya bahar. Chalte hue qadmon aur saans par dhyan dein. Agar chal na sakein to baith kar gardan, kandhe aur kamar aahista stretch karein. Zor lagana zaroori nahi. Agar mumkin ho to kisi ke saath chalna aur bhi acha hai, kyunke NHS ke mutabiq jismani activity akelepan ko kam karne me bhi madad kar sakti hai. Waqt: 5 se 10 minute (NHS ke mutabiq 10 minute se shuru karna kaafi hai). Yeh ilaaj nahi, ek chhoti technique hai jo kuch logon ko pur-sukoon mehsoos karne me madad karti hai.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
NHS aur Mayo Clinic dono kehte hain ke agar koi sehat ka masla ho, dawai chal rahi ho, ya kaafi arse se harkat na ki ho to naya exercise shuru karne se pehle doctor se poochein. Chakkar, seene me dard ya saans phoolne par foran ruk jayein (yeh Roohvi ki ehtiyat hai). Energy ya mood der tak kam rahe to doctor ya counsellor se baat karein.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Jin ko chot ya jismani pabandi ho unhe un ki halat ke mutabiq hi batayein.

ID: COP-09
Title: A short walk or stretch
Zubaan: English
Kab kaam aata: When the user feels restless or low, or has been sitting for long.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
NHS says shaking, stretching or moving can help release emotional tension, and a quick walk can be a mental break. Starting with 10 minutes a day is enough and every minute counts. Mayo Clinic says several 10-minute walks add up. Go at your own pace; if you cannot walk, stretch your neck, shoulders and back while seated. Walking with someone can be even better.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
NHS and Mayo Clinic both advise checking with a doctor before starting new exercise for anyone with a health condition, on medicines, or new to exercise. Stop if dizzy, in chest pain or short of breath (team caution).
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
For people with injury or physical limits, adapt to their situation.
