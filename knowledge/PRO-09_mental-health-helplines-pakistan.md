Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Neeche woh helplines hain jin ke numbers un ki apni sites par mile. Har ek ke saath likha hai ke kab check kiya gaya aur kya confirm nahi hua.

Umang: 0311-7786264. Site ke mutabiq 24/7 mental health helpline, clinical psychologists ke saath, pehla session free. Umang ki Contact page khud likhti hai ke woh aisi surat ke liye nahi jis me koi abhi khud ko nuqsan pahuncha raha ho; aisi halat me emergency service. Status: number VERIFIED (umang.com.pk/contact-us), check kiya 3 Oct 2026.

Rozan Counselling Helpline: 0304-111-1741. Islamabad ki NGO, naujawanon, auraton aur bachon par khaas tawajjo, aur gender-based violence par bhi. Timing UNVERIFIED: unki mukhtalif pages par alag alag likhi hain (kahin Monday-Friday subah 9 se shaam 5 baje, ek purani page par 10am-8pm saat din). Number VERIFIED, check kiya 3 Oct 2026; timing rozan.org par dobara dekhna zaroori hai.

Taskeen: 0316-8275336 (0316-TASKEEN). Free telephonic support, Monday-Saturday, 11 am se 11 pm. Woh diagnosis nahi karte. Status: VERIFIED (taskeen.org Find Help page), check kiya 3 Oct 2026.

Jo number ya timing aap confirm na kar saken use 'unverified' likhein.

Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
In helplines ki apni hudood hain. Agar jaan ka khatra hai ya koi abhi khud ko nuqsan pahuncha raha hai to 1122 ya 15 par call karein aur kisi apne ko paas bulayein.

Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Kisi aur number (maslan mashhoor lekin confirm na hua) ko list me na daalein. Umang ko 'emergency service' na likhein.
