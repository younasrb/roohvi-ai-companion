Title: Therapist ya counsellor kaise dhoondein
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: User ne madad lene ka soch liya hai magar pata nahi kahan se shuru kare.

Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Sahi insaan dhoondna aksar ek din me nahi hota, magar kai raaste hain jin me se aap koi bhi try kar sakte hain.

1. Family doctor: NIMH ke mutabiq primary care provider shuru-ati screening kar sakta hai aur psychologist, psychiatrist ya social worker ke paas bhej sakta hai. Appointment me apni pareshani ka zikr kiya ja sakta hai.

2. Directory: Taskeen ki site Pakistan Mental Health Coalition (PMHC) ki service directory ka link deti hai, jahan therapists, psychiatrists, counsellors, support groups aur rehab centres milte hain.

3. Helpline se rahnumai: Rozan ke mutabiq un ke paas lawyers, psychiatrists, psychologists aur doosre professionals ka referral network hai jo Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Karachi, Lahore waghaira me phaila hai.

4. Student hon to: NIMH ke mutabiq school ya college ka health center aur peer support bhi shuru karne ki jagah ho sakti hai.

Aap ek se zyada options dekh sakte hain. Kisi bhi jagah rabta karne se pehle yeh dekh lein ke woh service kis ke liye hai aur kis zubaan me hoti hai.

Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Agar intezar ke dauran halat bigadti lage ya khud ko nuqsan pahunchane ke khayal aayen to intezar na karein: 1122 ya 15 par rabta karein aur kisi apne ko paas bulayein.

Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Kisi khaas doctor, clinic ya paid app ka naam mat likhein jab tak official source se confirm na ho.
