Title: Grief
Zubaan: Simple English
Kab kaam aata: When a person experiences strong feelings after losing someone or something important.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Grief is a natural response to loss. Loss can include the death of a person, but it can also include the end of a relationship, losing a job or home, moving to a new place, or another important change.

Grief is different for each person. Someone may feel shocked, numb, very sad, tired, angry, or guilty. Feelings can change over time and may sometimes appear unexpectedly.

There is no single fixed way or exact timeline for grief. Talking with a trusted friend, family member, counsellor, or healthcare professional may help. Regular meals, physical activity, and small manageable daily tasks can also provide support.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If grief becomes very difficult to cope with, affects daily activities, keeps getting worse, or self-help does not improve things, talking to a healthcare professional or counsellor may help.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not treat grief as a mental illness or tell a person that they must follow fixed stages.
