ID: COP-01
Title: Box breathing (4 qadam wali saans)
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: Jab user tension, fikar ya jaldi ka bojh mehsoos kare aur ek chhoti, aasaan exercise try karna chahe.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Waqt: 2 se 5 minute. Box breathing me saans ke chaar hisse hote hain aur har hissa barabar lamba hota hai. NHS ke mutabiq: 4 ginti tak saans andar lein, 4 ginti tak rukein, 4 ginti tak saans bahar nikalein, aur 4 ginti tak phir rukein, phir dobara shuru karein. Aaram se seedhi kamar ke saath baith jayein aur kandhe dheele chhodein. NHS ka doosra page naak se andar aur munh se bahar saans lene aur saans ko zabardasti gehra na karne ko kehta hai. Aap kisi bhi waqt dheema ya band kar sakte hain; agar rokna mushkil lage to ginti chhoti kar lein ya rukne wala hissa chhod dein (yeh Roohvi ki ehtiyat hai, source ki nahi). 5 se 6 chakkar kaafi ho sakte hain. Yeh ilaaj nahi, ek chhoti technique hai jo kuch logon ko pur-sukoon mehsoos karne me madad karti hai. Aap ise ghabrahat ke dauran ya padhai se pehle try kar sakte hain.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Chakkar, halka sir ghoomna ya ghabrahat badhe to foran ruk jayein aur normal saans lein. Saans ya sehat ki koi medical takleef ho to pehle doctor se poochein (yeh Roohvi ki ehtiyat hai; NHS ke page par koi caution nahi tha). Agar ahsasat tez rahein ya koshish ke bawajood baar baar aate rahein to doctor ya counsellor se baat karna madadgar ho sakta hai.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Jis ko saans ya koi medical takleef ho, usay pehle doctor se poochne ko kahein; rukne wala hissa na dein.

ID: COP-01
Title: Box breathing
Zubaan: English
Kab kaam aata: When the user feels tense or worried and would like a simple exercise.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
2 to 5 minutes. NHS describes it as: breathe in for 4, hold for 4, breathe out for 4, hold for 4, then repeat. Sit comfortably. You can slow down or stop at any time. It is not a treatment, just a small technique that helps some people feel calmer.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Stop if you feel dizzy or more anxious. With a breathing or medical condition, ask a doctor first (team caution; the NHS page lists none). If feelings stay strong or keep coming back, see a doctor or counsellor.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not push this on a user with a breathing condition; offer COP-02 instead.
