Title: Mind and Body Connection
Zubaan: Simple English
Kab kaam aata: When a person notices physical changes along with stress or emotional feelings.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Mental and physical health are connected. Mental wellbeing is not only about feelings; physical health is also an important part of overall wellbeing.

Stress can affect both the mind and body. A person may feel worried, tense, or uneasy and may also notice physical symptoms such as headaches, body pain, or sleep difficulties. People can respond differently.

Daily habits can support both mental and physical wellbeing. Regular physical activity, healthy meals, enough sleep, hydration, and staying connected with trusted people can be helpful parts of a healthy routine.

Physical symptoms should not automatically be assumed to be caused by stress. Other physical causes are possible, so medical advice may be needed when there is concern.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If physical or emotional symptoms are severe, continue for a long time, affect daily activities, or cause concern about a physical cause, talking to a healthcare professional is important.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not diagnose or assume that every physical symptom is caused by stress or mental health.
