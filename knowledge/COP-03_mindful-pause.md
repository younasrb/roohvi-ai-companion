ID: COP-03
Title: Chhota mindful pause
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: Jab user thak gaya ho, khayalon me uljha ho ya din ke beech ek chhota sa thehrao chahta ho.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
NHS ke mutabiq mindfulness ka matlab hai har lamhe apne andar aur bahar jo ho raha hai us par dhyan dena. Yeh ek chhota pause hai, aap isay 1 se 3 minute ke liye bhi kar sakte hain (NHS ne koi waqt tay nahi kiya, yeh Roohvi ka aasaan suggestion hai). Jo kaam kar rahe hain usay thori der rok dein. Dhyan dein ke aap kya mehsoos kar rahe hain: kursi par jism ka wazan, hawa ka ehsaas, aap ke aas paas ki awaazein. Jab koi khayal aaye to usay roknay ki koshish na karein; use aise dekhein jaise woh zehan me aane wali ek cheez hai jo aati bhi hai aur chali bhi jati hai. NHS yeh bhi kehta hai ke khayal ya jazbe ko naam dena madadgar ho sakta hai, maslan chupke se kehna 'yeh ghabrahat hai'. Phir narmi se dhyan wapas abhi ke lamhe par le aayen. Aap isay roz ek tay waqt par, maslan chai peete hue ya chalte hue bhi kar sakte hain. Tareeqa: ruk jayein, dhyan dein, saans lein, phir agla chhota kaam chunein. Yeh ilaaj nahi, ek chhoti technique hai jo kuch logon ko pur-sukoon mehsoos karne me madad karti hai.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
NHS ke mutabiq mindfulness har kisi ke liye theek nahi; kuch logon ko isse behtar mehsoos nahi hota ya bura lagta hai. Agar pareshan karne wale khayal ya yaadein badh jayein to ruk jayein, COP-02 ya kisi halki activity par aa jayein, ya kisi se baat karein. Agar pause me soch aur bhaari ho jaye to ruk kar COP-02 try karein ya kisi se baat karein. Bhaari soch ya udaas mood kai din rahe to doctor ya counsellor se baat karein.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Jis waqt user bohat pareshan ho, us waqt COP-01 ya COP-02 behtar hain.

ID: COP-03
Title: Short mindful pause
Zubaan: English
Kab kaam aata: When the user feels tired or caught up in thoughts.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
NHS describes mindfulness as paying attention to what is going on inside and outside ourselves, moment by moment. Pause for a minute or two, notice your body, the air and the sounds around you. Let thoughts come and go instead of fighting them, and naming a feeling can help. (Duration is Roohvi's suggestion; NHS gives none.)
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
NHS says mindfulness is not right for everyone. If upsetting thoughts get stronger, stop and talk to someone.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
When very distressed, COP-01 or COP-02 fit better.
