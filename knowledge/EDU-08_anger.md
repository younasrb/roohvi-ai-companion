Title: Anger
Zubaan: Simple English
Kab kaam aata: When a person feels angry or irritated during conflict, pressure, unfair treatment, or a difficult situation.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Anger is a normal human feeling, and people can experience it in different ways. It can affect the body, thoughts, and behaviour.

Some people may notice a faster heartbeat, tense muscles, feeling hot, or tightness in the chest. A person may also feel tense, nervous, irritated, or unable to relax. Behaviour can include speaking loudly, ignoring others, or moving away from a situation.

Feeling angry and acting in a harmful way are different things. Taking some time before reacting may help. Some people may find counting, calming breathing, mindfulness, a short break, or walking useful. Talking to a trusted person can also help.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If anger is difficult to manage, affects daily life or relationships, or self-help does not help, talking to a healthcare professional or counsellor may be useful.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not diagnose a mental illness from anger alone.
