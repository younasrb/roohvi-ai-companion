ID: COP-08
Title: Sone se pehle ka sukoon
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: Jab user ko neend aane me dair lagti ho aur woh sone se pehle ki aadatein behtar karna chahe.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Waqt: 20 se 30 minute. NHS ke mutabiq roz taqreeban ek hi waqt par sone aur uthne se jism ko pata chalta hai ke sone ka waqt kab hai. Sone se taqreeban ek ghanta pehle phone, tablet ya computer se door ho jayein, kyunke screen ki neeli roshni neend aane me rukawat daal sakti hai. Chai, coffee aur kuch cold drinks me caffeine hoti hai jo jagaye rakhti hai. Sone ke qareeb bhaari khana, aur sone se 1 se 2 ghante pehle sharaab ya nicotine se bachein. Kamra shaant, andhera aur thanda rakhein; zaroorat ho to pardon, eye mask ya earplugs istemal karein. Agar lagbhag 20 minute baad bhi neend na aaye to bistar me parhe rehne ke bajaye uth kar koi pur-sukoon kaam karein, aur neend aane par wapas aayen. Din me jismani harkat neend behtar karti hai, magar sone ke bilkul qareeb kuch logon ki neend kharab kar sakti hai. Yeh sirf aadaton ki baat hai. Yeh ilaaj nahi, ek chhoti technique hai jo kuch logon ko pur-sukoon mehsoos karne me madad karti hai.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Agar neend ki mushkil lagataar rahe, ya din ke kaam mushkil ho jayen to doctor se baat karein. Dawai ke sawal doctor ya pharmacist ke liye hain.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Neend ki mushkil ko kisi bimari ka naam na dein.

ID: COP-08
Title: Sleep wind-down
Zubaan: English
Kab kaam aata: When the user has trouble falling asleep.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
NHS: go to bed and get up at similar times each day; avoid phones and screens for about an hour before sleep; watch caffeine, large late meals, and alcohol or nicotine 1 to 2 hours before bed; keep the bedroom quiet, dark and cool; if still awake after about 20 minutes, get up and do something relaxing, then return when sleepy.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If sleep problems continue or affect daily life, see a doctor. Medicine questions are for a doctor or pharmacist.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not give a medical label to sleep trouble.
