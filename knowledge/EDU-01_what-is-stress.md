Title: What is Stress?
Zubaan: Simple English
Kab kaam aata: When a person feels pressure because of a difficult situation, responsibilities, studies, work, or other life changes.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Stress is the mind and body's response to a challenge or demand. Everyone can feel stress sometimes. Stress is not always negative. A small amount of stress may help a person stay focused and complete a task. When stress becomes strong, a person may feel worried, tense, uneasy, or overwhelmed.

Stress can affect both the mind and body. Some people may have headaches, body pain, or difficulty sleeping. Everyone can respond to stress differently.

Some simple ways may help, such as writing feelings in a journal, being physically active, eating healthy meals, keeping a regular sleep routine, and talking to a trusted friend or family member.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If stress is difficult to manage, does not improve, or starts affecting daily life, studies, or work, talking to a healthcare professional or counsellor may help.
