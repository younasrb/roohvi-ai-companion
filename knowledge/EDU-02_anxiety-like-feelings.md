Title: Anxiety-like Feelings
Zubaan: Simple English
Kab kaam aata: When a person feels worry, fear, uneasiness, tension, or nervousness.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Anxiety-like feelings can happen as part of the body's and mind's response to stress. Stress is often connected to an external situation or problem. Anxiety can sometimes continue even when there is no immediate danger.

A person may feel worried, tense, uneasy, or have difficulty sleeping. Some people may also have headaches or body pain. These feelings are not the same for everyone.

Helpful self-care may include writing feelings down, relaxation activities, physical activity, healthy meals, a regular sleep routine, and talking to a trusted person.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If worry or anxiety-like feelings continue, become difficult to manage, or affect daily activities, talking to a healthcare professional may help.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not diagnose an anxiety disorder or another condition.
