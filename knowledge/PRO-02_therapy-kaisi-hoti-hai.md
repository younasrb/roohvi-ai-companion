Title: Therapy ya counselling kaisi hoti hai
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: User ghabrata hai ke pehli mulaqat me kya hoga.

Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Pehli dafa therapy ke baare me sochna ghabrahat wali baat ho sakti hai, is liye pehle se jaan lena madadgar hai. NHS ki Talking Therapies services ke mutabiq pehli mulaqat aksar ek assessment hoti hai: aap apni mushkilat batate hain, phir milkar tay hota hai ke kaunsi madad munasib rahegi. Yeh mulaqat kai jagah phone par bhi hoti hai.

Aap se kuch sawalnamay (questionnaires) bharwaye ja sakte hain. Un me sahi ya ghalat jawab nahi hote.

Baad ki sessions me practitioner aam tor par session ka maqsad batata hai aur aap ko bhi apni baat rakhne ka mauqa milta hai. Kuch therapies me sessions ke darmiyan chhote kaam (maslan diary ya mashq) diye jate hain taake naye tareeqe asal zindagi me azmaye ja saken.

Aap keh sakte hain ke kisi waqiye ki tafseel par abhi baat nahi karni. Aap break maang sakte hain, aur mulaqat chhor kar ja bhi sakte hain.

Yeh sab UK ki services ka tajurba hai; Pakistan me tareeqa alag ho sakta hai. Taskeen jaisi helplines tele-consultation deti hain aur khud likhti hain ke woh formal assessment ya diagnosis nahi karti. Shuru me yeh poochna theek hai: 'yeh session kis tarah ki hogi?'

Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Agar session ke baad bechaini bohat badh jaye ya khud ko nuqsan pahunchane ke khayal aayen to apne therapist ya kisi apne ko foran batayein; jaan ka khatra ho to 1122 ya 15 par call karein.

Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Un logon ko tasalli ke taur par 'therapy se sab theek ho jata hai' na kahein; koi guarantee nahi di ja sakti.
