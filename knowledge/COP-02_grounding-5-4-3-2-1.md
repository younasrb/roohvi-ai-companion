ID: COP-02
Title: 5-4-3-2-1 grounding
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: Jab user ka zehan tez chal raha ho, woh pareshan ya ghabraya hua ho aur 'abhi' me wapas aana chahe.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Waqt: 3 se 5 minute. Waqt: 2 se 5 minute. Jahan bhi hon, ek lamha ruk kar apne hawaas par dhyan dein. Pehle 5 cheezein dekhein aur unka naam lein, maslan koi parinda, darakht, chhat ka koi nishan ya deewar par lagi tasveer. Phir 4 cheezein jinhe aap chhoo sakte hain, maslan apne baal, hath ya jis kursi par baithe hain. Phir 3 awaazein sunein, maslan ghadi ki tik tik, gaari ya kutte ka bhaunkna. Phir 2 cheezein jin ki khushboo aap soongh sakte hain, maslan sabun ya apne kapde. Aakhir me 1 cheez jis ka zaiqa aap le sakein, maslan chewing gum ya chai. Dheere dheere karein, jaldi nahi. Mind ke mutabiq panic ke waqt hawaas par dhyan dena madadgar ho sakta hai, maslan pudina wali toffee (mint candy) ya gum chakhna, ya koi naram cheez chhoona. Maqsad yeh hai ke dhyan tez soch se hat kar is waqt ki cheezon par aaye. Yeh ilaaj nahi, ek chhoti technique hai jo kuch logon ko pur-sukoon mehsoos karne me madad karti hai.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Agar yeh karte hue ghabrahat badhe, ya user ko khud se ya aas paas se kata hua mehsoos ho aur woh theek na ho, to ruk jayein aur kisi bharosemand insaan ya professional se baat karne ki taraf layein. Zaiqe wali cheez sirf wohi lein jo user ke liye mehfooz ho (allergy ka khayal rakhein). Agar apne jism ya aas paas par dhyan dena pareshan kare to sirf kamre ki cheezon ko dekhein. Ghabrahat ya haqeeqat se kata hua mehsoos hona baar baar ho to doctor ya counsellor se baat karein.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Jis user ko pehle se kisi yaad ya halat ki wajah se bohat takleef ho, usay isme majboor na karein.

2 سے 5 منٹ۔ 5 چیزیں دیکھیں، 4 چھو کر محسوس کریں، 3 آوازیں سنیں، 2 خوشبوئیں سونگھیں اور 1 ذائقہ لیں (مثلاً چیونگم یا چائے)۔ آہستہ آہستہ کریں۔ مقصد توجہ تیز سوچ سے ہٹا کر اس لمحے کی چیزوں پر لانا ہے۔

ID: COP-02
Title: 5-4-3-2-1 grounding
Zubaan: English
Kab kaam aata: When the user's mind is racing and they want to come back to the present.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
2 to 5 minutes. Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell and 1 you can taste (for example chewing gum or tea). Go slowly. The aim is to move attention from racing thoughts to what is around you now.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If it makes distress worse, or the user feels cut off from themselves or their surroundings and it does not ease, stop and suggest a trusted person or a professional. Use only taste items that are safe for the user.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not push it on someone who is very upset by a memory.
