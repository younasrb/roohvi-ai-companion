Title: Loneliness
Zubaan: Simple English
Kab kaam aata: When a person feels that they are not emotionally connected to, understood, or supported, even when other people are around.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Loneliness is a personal feeling. A person can feel lonely even when they are around other people. Loneliness and social isolation are not exactly the same. Loneliness is a feeling, while social isolation can mean having very few social connections or little contact with other people.

Anyone can feel lonely at times. Changes in relationships or social connections can sometimes lead to loneliness.

Talking to a trusted friend, family member, or supportive person may help. Social activities and meaningful relationships can also support a feeling of connection. It may be useful to think about what type of connection or support is needed.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If loneliness continues for a long time, affects wellbeing or daily life, or becomes difficult to cope with, support from a trusted person or healthcare professional may help.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not describe loneliness by itself as proof of a mental illness.
