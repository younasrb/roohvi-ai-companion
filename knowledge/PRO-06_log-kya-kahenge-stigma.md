Title: 'Log kya kahenge' ka dar aur stigma
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: User madad lene se is liye jhijakta hai ke log ya khandan kya sochenge.

Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
'Log kya kahenge' ka dar aam hai, aur is me akele aap nahi hain. WHO Europe ke mutabiq stigma mental health me sab se bari rukawaton me se ek hai. Ghalat fehmi aur galat maloomat se logon ke saath na-insafi ho sakti hai, jise stigma aur discrimination kehte hain.

Taskeen, jo Pakistan ki ek mental health NGO hai, apne Public Awareness department ke zariye isi mamle par kaam karti hai: logon ko maloomat dena, stigma kam karna, aur unhe madad tak pahunchne me aasani dena.

Chhote qadam jo aap soch sakte hain:
- Pehle apne dar ko maan lein. Yeh dar ghalat nahi, bohat se log aisa hi mehsoos karte hain.
- Pehla rabta aisi jagah se karein jahan aap ki pehchaan par sawal na uthe, maslan phone helpline.
- Kisi aise ek insaan se baat karne ka soch sakte hain jis par aap bharosa karte hon. NHS Every Mind Matters ke mutabiq kisi se apni baat kehna cheezon ko dusri nazar se dekhne me madad kar sakta hai.

Madad lena kisi ko 'kamzor' nahi banata; yeh aap ka apna faisla hai ke aap kab aur kis se baat karein.

Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Agar dar ki wajah se aap bohat dino se kisi se baat nahi kar paa rahe aur halat bigad rahi hai, ya khud ko nuqsan pahunchane ke khayal aate hain, to kisi apne ya helpline se rabta karein; jaan ka khatra ho to 1122 ya 15.

Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Mazhabi ya khandani aqeedon ko ghalat na kahein, aur kisi ko sharminda na karein. Yeh baat Maria ki LNG entries me bhi dekhi jaye.
