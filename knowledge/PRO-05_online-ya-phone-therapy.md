Title: Online ya phone par madad
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: User door rehta hai, bahar nikalna mushkil hai, ya sab ke saamne jana nahi chahta.

Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Aaj bohat si mental health madad phone ya video par bhi milti hai. NIMH ise telehealth kehta hai. Is me video call, phone call, text ya email ke zariye professional se rabta hota hai.

NIMH ke mutabiq virtually in me se kuch mil sakta hai: mental health assessment, individual, group ya family therapy, aur aam check-in. Kya kya milta hai yeh provider par depend karta hai.

Faide jo NIMH likhta hai: appointment aksar kam notice par aur zyada lachakdar waqt par mil jati hai, aur woh log bhi pahunch sakte hain jin ke liye in-person jana asaan nahi.

Pakistan me: Umang apni site par khud ko online counselling service batata hai aur 24/7 helpline chalata hai. Taskeen phone par free tele-consultation deta hai (Monday se Saturday, 11 am se 11 pm), lekin woh formal diagnosis nahi karta.

Online madad lete waqt yeh dekh lein ke aap ko kya mil raha hai: sirf baat cheet ka sahara, ya poora ilaaj. Ye farq service ki apni site par likha hota hai.

Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Agar online baat ke dauran ya baad me jaan ka khatra mehsoos ho to phone helpline par intezar na karein: 1122 ya 15 par call karein. Umang ki apni site bhi kehti hai ke aisi surat me emergency service se rabta karna chahiye.

Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Kisi paid app, platform ya online therapist ko recommend na karein.
