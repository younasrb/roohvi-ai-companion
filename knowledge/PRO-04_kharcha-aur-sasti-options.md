Title: Kharcha aur free ya sasti madad
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: User kehta hai 'mere paas paise nahi hain' ya kharche ki fikr hai.

Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Kharcha kai logon ke liye madad lene me rukawat banta hai. Neeche kuch jagahein hain jin ki tafseel un ki apni sites par likhi hai.

Umang: apni site par 24/7 mental health helpline likhta hai, aur 'pehla session free' ka zikr karta hai.

Taskeen: apni site ke mutabiq free-of-cost telephonic mental health support deta hai, Monday se Saturday, subah 11 se raat 11 baje tak.

Rozan: unki site ke FAQ me likha hai ke counselling ki koi fees nahi, magar mobile call ke aam charges lagte hain. Yeh page purana ho sakta hai, is liye Rozan se confirm karna behtar hai.

In services ki apni hudood hain. Maslan Taskeen likhta hai ke woh formal psychological evaluation ya diagnosis nahi karta. Phir bhi yeh pehla qadam ho sakte hain, aur zaroorat par aage kahan jana hai is par rahnumai bhi maangi ja sakti hai.

Private clinics ki fees ya sarkari hospitals ki psychiatry OPD ke baare me is entry me koi raqam ya number nahi diya gaya, kyun ke official source se confirm nahi hua.

Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Kharche ki wajah se madad ko taalna theek nahi jab jaan ka khatra ho: aisi surat me 1122 ya 15 par rabta karein, aur kisi apne ko paas bulayein.

Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Koi fees ka andaza, discount ya 'muft ilaaj' ka wada mat likhein jo official site par na ho.
