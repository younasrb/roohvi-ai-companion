Title: Emergency numbers: Rescue 1122 aur Police 15
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: User ya uska koi apna foran khatre me hai, ya poochta hai 'emergency me kise bulayein'.

Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Agar kisi ki jaan ko abhi khatra hai, ya koi zakhmi hai, ya aap khud ko nuqsan pahunchane ke qareeb mehsoos kar rahe hain, to helpline se pehle emergency service se rabta karna zaroori hai.

Rescue 1122: Punjab Emergency Service ki official site (rescue.gov.pk) 1122 ko emergency number batati hai. Yeh Punjab hukumat ka idara hai. Doosre sooba me bhi 1122 naam ki service batayi jati hai, lekin us ka official page confirm nahi hua, isliye woh UNVERIFIED hai.

Police 15: Punjab Police ki official page par Police Emergency ka number 15 likha hai. Karachi Police ki site par 'Madadgar 15' ka zikr hai jo Karachi me 24/7 helpline hai. Punjab Police ki page purani ho sakti hai, check kiya 3 Oct 2026.

Jab aap call karein: apni jagah (shehar, ilaaqa, qareebi nishani) aur kya hua hai, saaf batayein. Phone par kisi apne ko saath rakhein. Agar aap kisi aur ke liye call kar rahe hain to us ke saath rahein jab tak madad na pahunch jaye.

Mental health helplines (Umang, Rozan, Taskeen) emergency service ki jagah nahi leti.

Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Yeh poori entry hi 'kab madad lena zaroori hai' ke liye hai: jaan ka khatra, chot, ya koi khud ko nuqsan pahuncha raha hai to foran 1122 ya 15.

Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Kisi tareeqe, cheez ya jagah ka zikr mat karein. Sirf madad ka rasta batayein. Dawai ya ilaaj ki baat mat karein.
