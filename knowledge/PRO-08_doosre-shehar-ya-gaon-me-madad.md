ID: PRO-08
Title: Doosre shehar ya gaon me madad kaise milti hai
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: User bade shehar se door hai ya paas koi mental health service nahi.

Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Agar aap ke qareeb koi therapist ya clinic nahi, to bhi kuch raaste ho sakte hain.

Phone ya online: NIMH ke mutabiq virtual care un logon tak bhi pahunch sakti hai jin ke liye in-person jana asaan nahi. Umang apni site par 24/7 online counselling aur helpline batata hai. Taskeen phone par free tele-consultation deta hai, Monday se Saturday, subah 11 se raat 11 baje.

Khat ya email: Rozan ki site ke mutabiq jahan telephonic service nahi pahunchti wahan 'letter counselling' di jati hai, aur email counselling ka pata bhi unki site par hai (helpline@rozan.org).

Referral: Rozan ke mutabiq un ke paas Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Karachi, Lahore waghaira me psychiatrists, psychologists aur doosre professionals ka referral network hai. Aap helpline se pooch sakte hain ke aap ke ilaqe ke qareeb kya mil sakta hai.

Directory: PMHC ki service directory me therapists, counsellors aur support groups ki list hai.

Yaad rahe ke yeh sab services alag alag waqt par kaam karti hain. Pehle unki site par timing dekh lein.

Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Agar halat emergency jaisi ho to helpline ka intezar na karein: 1122 ya 15 par call karein, aur kisi apne ko foran paas bulayein.

Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Kisi shehar ke clinic ya doctor ka naam ya number na likhein jo official site se confirm na ho.
