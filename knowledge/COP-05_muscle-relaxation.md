ID: COP-05
Title: Muscles ko dheela karna (progressive muscle relaxation)
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: Jab user ka jism akda hua ho, kandhe ya pet tight hon aur woh relax hona chahe.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Waqt: 10 se 15 minute. Mind ke mutabiq araam se late jayein ya seedhi kamar ke saath baith jayein, paon zameen par rakhein, aankhein band karein ya door kisi nuqte par nazar rakhein. Pairon ki ungliyon se shuru karein: unhe thori der ke liye kas lein, phir chhod dein aur farq mehsoos karein. Mind kehta hai ke saans andar lete hue muscles kasein aur saans bahar nikalte hue unhe dheela chhod dein. Phir isi tarah upar badhein: ranein, pet, kandhe aur hath, har hisse ko ek ek karke kasein aur chhodein. Khatam karne par thori der araam se rahein, phir aahista harkat shuru karein, aur jab tayyar hon to dheere se khade hon. Kasna halka rakhein, dard tak nahi. Mind kehta hai ke regular practice se zyada faida hota hai, aur shaant, aaramdeh jagah chunna madadgar hai. Yeh ilaaj nahi, ek chhoti technique hai jo kuch logon ko pur-sukoon mehsoos karne me madad karti hai.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Jis hisse me dard ya chot ho usay na kasein (yeh Roohvi team ki ehtiyat hai, Mind ne nahi likha). Agar relax hona namumkin lage ya takleef barqarar rahe to kisi professional se baat karna madadgar hai. Lagataar akdan, dard ya bechaini der tak rahe to doctor se baat karein.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Jin ko chot, muscle ya jodon ka dard ho, unhe sirf wahi hisse batayein jahan dard na ho.

ID: COP-05
Title: Muscle relaxation
Zubaan: English
Kab kaam aata: When the user's body feels tense and they want to relax.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Per Mind: lie down or sit with your back straight, feet on the floor. Start with your toes: tense as you breathe in, relax as you breathe out. Work up through thighs, stomach, shoulders and hands, one at a time. Rest for a moment, then move slowly and stand up when ready. Tense lightly.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Do not tense a painful or injured area (team caution, not from Mind). If relaxing feels impossible or symptoms continue, talk to a professional.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
For people with injuries or joint pain, use only pain-free areas.
