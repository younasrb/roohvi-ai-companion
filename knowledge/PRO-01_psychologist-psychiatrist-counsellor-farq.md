Title: Psychologist, psychiatrist aur counsellor me farq
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: User poochta hai ke kis se milna chahiye, ya in naamon me uljhan hai.

Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Mushkil waqt me madad dene wale kai log hote hain, aur un ke naam aksar ek dusre se mil jate hain. NHS Health Careers ke mutabiq in kaamon me overlap bhi hota hai, magar mote tor par yeh farq samajhna madadgar hai.

Psychologist: aam tor par medical doctor nahi hote. Woh alag shaakhon me kaam karte hain, jaise clinical ya counselling psychology.

Psychiatrist: yeh medical doctor hote hain jinhon ne psychiatry me specialisation ki hoti hai. Dawai ke baare me sirf doctor ya pharmacist batayenge.

Psychotherapist: woh hota hai jo psychiatrist, psychologist ya kisi aur professional hone ke baad psychotherapy ki mazeed training le. NIMH ke mutabiq baat cheet wali therapy bohat se alag background ke log dete hain.

Counsellor aur therapist ke naam kai jagah ek dusre ki jagah bolay jate hain. Is liye naam ke saath yeh jaan lena behtar hai ke un ki training kya hai.

Kahan se shuru karein: NIMH kehta hai ke primary care provider (maslan family doctor) shuru-ati jaanch kar ke psychologist, psychiatrist ya social worker ke paas bhej sakta hai. Pakistan me aap PMHC directory bhi dekh sakte hain; unki FAQ me likha hai ke psychiatrist ke liye MBBS aur MCPS/FCPS (psychiatry) chahiye.

Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
NIMH ke mutabiq agar mushkil ya takleef 2 hafte ya zyada rahe (neend, bhook, tawajjo, dilchaspi ya rozmarra kaam par asar) to professional se milna munasib hai. Jaan ka khatra ho to fori 1122 ya 15 par rabta karein aur kisi apne ko paas bulayein.

Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Kisi ko yeh na batayein ke 'aap ko falan bimari hai'. Yeh entry sirf roles samjhane ke liye hai.
