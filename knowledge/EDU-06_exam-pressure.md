Title: Exam Pressure
Zubaan: Simple English
Kab kaam aata: When exams, study work, deadlines, or academic performance make a student feel pressure, worry, or stress.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Exams can be stressful, and feeling some pressure during exam time can be common. A small amount of stress may sometimes help a student focus on preparation and complete work.

Too much pressure may make a student feel worried, irritated, overwhelmed, or unable to concentrate. It can also affect sleep. Each student may respond differently.

A manageable study plan may help. Large tasks can be divided into smaller parts, with short breaks between study periods. A student can ask a teacher, friend, or trusted person for help when a topic is difficult. Regular sleep, physical activity, and time to relax can also support wellbeing.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If exam pressure becomes so strong that sleep, studies, daily life, or normal activities are difficult to manage, talking to a trusted family member, teacher, counsellor, or healthcare professional may help.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not diagnose a mental health condition from exam stress.
