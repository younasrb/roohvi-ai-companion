Title: Sleep Problems
Zubaan: Simple English
Kab kaam aata: When a person has difficulty falling asleep, wakes often during the night, wakes too early, or feels tired during the day because of poor sleep.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Sometimes poor sleep is common, and a few sleepless nights are usually not a serious concern. However, regular poor sleep can affect mood and daily life.

A person may have trouble falling asleep, wake several times during the night, or wake early and be unable to sleep again. Poor sleep can leave a person tired, sleepy, or irritable and may make concentration and daily tasks harder.

A regular sleep routine may help. A person can try going to bed and waking at similar times, relaxing before bed, keeping the room comfortable, avoiding caffeine later in the day, and putting the phone away at bedtime. Healthy adults usually need about 7–9 hours of sleep.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If sleep problems happen regularly, affect daily activities, or do not improve after changing sleep habits, talking to a healthcare professional may help.
