Title: Feeling Sad
Zubaan: Simple English
Kab kaam aata: When a person feels sad, low, worried, stressed, or has less interest in usual activities.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
Everyone can feel sad or low sometimes. Mild sadness, stress, or worry can happen during difficult times. A person may also feel tired, have less energy, have trouble concentrating, or notice changes in sleep or appetite.

Feeling sad does not automatically mean that a person has a mental health condition. It may help to talk to a trusted friend or family member, keep a regular routine, stay physically active, and make time for low-stress activities.

NIMH notes that self-care can help with mild symptoms. If symptoms do not improve or become worse, professional support can be considered.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If symptoms are severe or last for 2 weeks or more, or if daily tasks and usual activities become difficult, talking to a healthcare professional is important.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not diagnose depression from sadness alone.
