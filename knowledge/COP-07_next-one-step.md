ID: COP-07
Title: Agla ek qadam
Zubaan: Roman Urdu
Kab kaam aata: Jab user ko kaam, padhai ya masla bohat bada lage aur samajh na aaye ke kahan se shuru kare.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
NHS ke 'worry tree' tareeqe me pehle yeh dekha jata hai ke fikar aisi hai jis par aap kuch kar sakte hain ya nahi. Jis par kar sakte hain, uske liye teen sawal poochein: main kya karunga? kaise karunga? kab karunga? Isi ko Roohvi 'agla ek qadam' kehti hai. Pehle masle ko ek jumle me likhein. Phir sirf ek aisa chhota hissa chunein jo aap aaj ya kal kar sakein, aur uska waqt tay kar lein. Poora masla ek saath hal karne ki zaroorat nahi. Agar woh qadam bhi bara lage to usay aur chhota kar dein. Jo fikar aap ke qaboo me nahi hai, NHS ke mutabiq use maan lena aur dhyan kisi aur taraf le jana madadgar ho sakta hai. Aap kisi se madad bhi maang sakte hain. Waqt: 1 se 5 minute. Aap yeh try kar sakte hain. Qadam bohat chhota rakhein. Yeh ilaaj nahi, ek chhoti technique hai jo kuch logon ko pur-sukoon mehsoos karne me madad karti hai.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
Agar masla kisi ki hifazat ya jaan ka ho, ya user bohat bechain ho, to yeh tareeqa kaafi nahi; kisi bharosemand insaan ya madad ka raasta dikhayein (SAF-06). Agar yeh chhota qadam bhi na ho sake to koi baat nahi; kisi se baat karna madadgar ho sakta hai. Kai din tak sab kuch bhaari lage to doctor ya counsellor se baat karein.
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Jo user pehle se bohat thaka ya pareshan ho usay lambi planning par majboor na karein.

ID: COP-07
Title: Just the next step
Zubaan: English
Kab kaam aata: When a task or problem feels too big to start.
Steps ya samjhana (150-300 words):
NHS's worry-tree approach first asks whether you can do something about the worry. If you can, answer three questions: what would you do, how, and when. Roohvi calls this 'the next one step'. Pick just one small part and fix a time. Accept worries you cannot control and move your focus on.
Kab ruk jana ya madad lena zaroori hai:
If someone's safety or life is at risk, or the user is very distressed, this is not enough; show the path to help (SAF-06).
Kisko nahi dena (agar koi ho):
Do not push long planning on someone already very tired or upset.
